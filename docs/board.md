Engineering Report: The Hoshen 12-Code Matrix Architecture

The **Hoshen Breastplate is a 12-bit multi-state analog processing matrix** that relies on crystal geometry and material weight to compute states. While a standard chessboard operates on a binary grid (black and white cells creating distinct voltage differences), the Hoshen uses a **Base-12 multi-frequency code**. Each gemstone node acts as an independent frequency-dependent variable capacitor, mapping physical weight, dimensions, and density to explicit logical operations.

* * * * *

1\. The 12-Code Crystalline Logic System

Instead of binary states (0 and 1) or hexadecimal intervals, the system operates on a **12-axis resonant code**.

-   **Frequency Mapping:** Each stone possesses a specific atomic lattice spacing. This spacing dictates its baseline electrical potential under kinetic pressure.
-   **The Weight Variable:** The physical mass and volume of each crystal govern its energy storage capacity (*capacitance*).
-   **The Aperture Width:** The surface diameter determines the current throughput capacity (*impedance*).
-   **The Crossbar Routing:** Perpendicular woven channels (horizontal and vertical threads) route these 12 distinct frequency signatures into unified wavefronts.
